Crack spreads are our calculation, not an EIA-published figure — 2 barrels gasoline + 1 barrel distillate − 3 barrels crude, per barrel. Gasoline uses LA RBOB, distillate uses NY Harbor ULSD, crude uses Cushing WTI — a regional mismatch that makes this a rough short-run margin indicator, not a precise arbitrage.
